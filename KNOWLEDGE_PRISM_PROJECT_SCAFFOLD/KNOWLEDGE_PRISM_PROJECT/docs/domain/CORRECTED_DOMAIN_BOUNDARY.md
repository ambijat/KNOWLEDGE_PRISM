# Corrected Domain-Boundary Document

## Status of this document

This is a corrected boundary statement for the inherited Claude Science corpus-mapping project. It does not treat the previous domain definition as final. It separates mapped evidence from verified textual evidence and narrows the project into a researchable domain.

## What can be safely claimed now

The existing artifacts support the following inventory-level claims:

1. A Zotero bibliographic layer exists and contains 1,841 entries.
2. A Recoll-derived manifest exists and contains 12,594 path-level document records.
3. A direct SOLEMON crawl exists and contains 35,178 path-level records.
4. A preliminary path-union register can be generated from Recoll and SOLEMON artifacts.
5. A 392-row bridge-concept table exists, but its conceptual claims remain unverified unless checked against actual text.
6. The corpus contains a visible empirical-geopolitical layer and a visible methodological/knowledge-structure layer.

## What cannot yet be safely claimed

The following claims are not yet permitted as final scholarly statements:

1. That the entire corpus has been read.
2. That folder names accurately represent intellectual categories.
3. That all duplicates have been removed.
4. That the `bridge_concepts.csv` theses accurately represent the books.
5. That the corpus has a final ontology.
6. That the post-2021 empirical literature gap has been fully established.
7. That external literature should now be pulled.

## Corrected domain boundary

The domain should not be defined as the whole personal library. It should also not be defined as all geopolitics, all IR theory, all AI, all semiotics, and all Central Asian studies at once.

The corrected domain is:

> **Computational and interpretive mapping of geopolitical knowledge structures in International Relations, using Afghanistan-Central Asia/Eurasia as the empirical core and systems theory, semiotics, network analysis, grounded theory, and AI-assisted ontology-building as the methodological apparatus.**

## Domain components

### 1. Empirical core

The empirical core is restricted to:

- Afghanistan and the Taliban political-security complex
- Central Asia and Eurasian regional security
- South Asia as it intersects with Afghanistan and Central Asia
- China, Russia, India, Pakistan, and the United States as relevant actors
- BRI/OBOR/CPEC, connectivity, energy corridors, and regional infrastructure when they structure the Eurasian security space

### 2. Theoretical core

The theoretical core includes:

- International Relations theory
- realism and neorealism
- constructivism
- English School
- regional security complex theory
- critical geopolitics
- geopolitics and political geography

### 3. Methodological core

The methodological core includes:

- general systems theory
- semiotics and visual/cartographic sign systems
- grounded theory and inductive category formation
- social network analysis
- computational modelling
- AI-assisted ontology-building
- corpus mapping and knowledge representation

### 4. Excluded or peripheral zones

The following are outside the core unless a specific bridge is established:

- general physics, chemistry, biology, medical, or technical manuals
- ordinary administrative files
- unrelated teaching dumps
- generic philosophy not connected to knowledge representation or IR theory
- regional studies material not connected to Afghanistan-Central Asia/Eurasian security or the knowledge-structure problem

## Evidence ladder for domain claims

| Claim type | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Corpus exists | Manifest or crawl record |
| Item exists | Path, Zotero entry, or Recoll record |
| Item is probably relevant | Title, folder, metadata, or search snippet |
| Item is domain-core | Frontmatter or chapter sample |
| Item contributes a concept | Textual passage supporting the concept |
| Concept belongs in ontology | Repeated support across multiple verified texts |
| Domain gap exists | Verified internal absence plus targeted external comparison |

## Revised research question

A defensible central research question is:

> How can a dispersed scholarly corpus be transformed into a verifiable research ontology for mapping geopolitical knowledge structures in International Relations, with Afghanistan-Central Asia/Eurasia as the empirical field and systems/semiotic/network/AI methods as the modelling apparatus?

## Corrected conclusion

The inherited project has successfully produced corpus reconnaissance. It has not yet produced a final ontology. The next phase is not expansion but discipline: deduplication, evidence grading, verification, and narrowing.

The project should proceed as an auditable scholarly-corpus ontology, not as a large unverified AI-generated domain map.

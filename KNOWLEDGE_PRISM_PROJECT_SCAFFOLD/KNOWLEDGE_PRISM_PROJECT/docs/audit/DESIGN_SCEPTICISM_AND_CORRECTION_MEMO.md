# DESIGN SCEPTICISM AND CORRECTION MEMO

This memo records the methodological corrections made after taking over the project.

## Flaw 1: folder names were being treated as ontology

Correction: folder names are clues. They are not ontology classes until verified against actual contents.

## Flaw 2: metadata inference sounded like full reading

Correction: all claims must carry evidence grades. Metadata-only claims are allowed only for inventory, triage, and hypothesis formation.

## Flaw 3: the domain was too broad

Correction: the domain has been narrowed to computational and interpretive mapping of geopolitical knowledge structures in IR, with Afghanistan--Central Asia/Eurasia as the empirical core.

## Flaw 4: external literature was being considered too early

Correction: no external paper-pulling until the internal corpus is cleaned and gaps are known.

## Flaw 5: large corpus counts created false confidence

Correction: corpus counts must be separated into raw records, unique path records, duplicate candidates, core candidates, peripheral material, and noise.

## Flaw 6: bridge concepts were over-trusted

Correction: `bridge_concepts.csv` is now a verification queue, not a verified knowledge base.

Problem

Search finds files. Research still needs judgment.

Researchers have PDFs, Zotero libraries, policy reports, notes, and grey literature scattered across folders. Search can surface documents, but it cannot tell what is trustworthy, what is relevant, what is still only a clue, what needs expert review, or what can enter an explanatory graph.

Who it is for

For people who must defend what they know.

Built for PhD scholars, supervisors, research centres, think tanks, library heads, and policy research teams who need more than a document dump. They need a map of claims, evidence, uncertainty, and interpretive value.

Method

A research exoskeleton, not an AI replacement.

The scholar keeps the question and the final judgment. The Human Workbench turns source clues into guarded outputs without silently promoting them into verified ontology.

Map the archive

Inventory the corpus and expose what is only metadata.

Test claims

Sample text before treating a generated claim as usable.

Grade evidence

Keep each claim tied to its current level of support.

Interpret function

Ask what the text does for the research question.

Build graph

Connect works, concepts, interactions, and cautions.

Export brief

Carry the review state forward with provenance intact.

Trust layer

Every claim carries its luggage.

Every claim keeps its source, evidence grade, relevance decision, functional interpretation, and review status. Metadata can suggest a path, but it is not treated as scholarship. The graph grows only where the evidence can follow.

Human input becomes guarded output

The Human Workbench accepts a research question, source clue, evidence grade, and functional role, then exports a review note while keeping ontology promotion blocked until provenance is strong.

Public overview first

Visitors begin with the value, then move into the pilot demonstration, method, evidence, and expert machinery only when needed.

Advanced mode is separated

The Expert Dashboard remains available for deep inspection, but it is clearly marked as the advanced evidence cockpit.

Private material stays private

The public demo uses safe exports only. Raw retrieval outputs, raw corpus files, local paths, secrets, and database files are not served.

Machinery, when needed

Start with value. Drill down only when you need the machinery.
